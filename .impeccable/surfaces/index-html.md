# Surface: index.html (home, single page)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: couples, families and groups comparing lodging in Tafí del Valle, mostly on phones from Instagram or Maps. Action: check availability and rates by WhatsApp (dates + guests prefilled), in ES or EN. Proof: Google 4.9 (111), Booking sub-scores 9.5–10, TripAdvisor 5.0, what guests praise. Constraints: no fixed prices, no invented quotes or claims, cabin photos arrive later in `img/` from the owner's Google profile; the page must hold without them.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a topographic sheet of the Tafí valley. Scrolling descends from the sheet's title to the plot where La Madrina sits above Dique La Angostura. It refuses the category default: full-bleed photo hero, serif name, floating booking bar.

OWN-WORLD: Survey-sheet ground (#f4f3ee) under hypsometric tints that own whole sections: valley green #2f4a35, highland ochre #c9b98f, contour sienna #9a5a2c. Dique blue #2d6f9a is reserved by law for water and the contact action. Archivo throughout: wide caps tracked for place labels, italic for water names, condensed for figures. Hairline frames with sheet neatlines and coordinate ticks; a legend of drawn symbols defines amenities once.

STORY: The visitor sees exactly where this is and what view it owns, picks the cabin category for their group, trusts the guest scores, and sends a prefilled WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width sheet. Live SVG contour field covers the frame, the dique as a blue italic-labeled body lower left, La Madrina's mark with coordinates and a leader line. At left, the name at display scale over a two-line promise. Bottom right, the title-block cartouche holds the working form: arrival, departure, guests, "Consultar por WhatsApp". Top: the sheet's edge strip with nav and an ES/EN switch.

FORM: Carta de altura, position 4 on my ordered list, seed:1f3b84a01d8f516c.

Signature interaction: the Ruta 307 line draws one step ahead of the scroll down the page's margin. Selecting a cabin category places a trig-point triangle marker on it and updates the WhatsApp message. Raises: dique blue reserved for water + action; state as mark; amenities as map legend; live check-in status in Tucumán time; reading copy always on clean sheet panels; route drawn ahead of scroll.
