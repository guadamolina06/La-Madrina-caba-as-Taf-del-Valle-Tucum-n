# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS (plus a small vanilla JS file for language switching and motion). No framework, no build step. Deploys as static files (GitHub Pages compatible).

## Users

Travelers planning a stay in Tafí del Valle, Tucumán: couples (2–4), families (4–6) and larger groups or extended families (6–8, some units up to 10). Mostly Argentine visitors from Tucumán and other provinces, plus foreign tourists (the complex attends in Spanish and English). They usually arrive from Instagram, Google Maps or a booking platform, often on a phone, comparing a few lodging options in the valley and wanting to confirm: what the cabin is like, the view, what is included, where it is, and how to book directly.

## Product Purpose

Official single-page website for La Madrina Cabañas de altura (also known as Apart Hotel La Madrina). It presents the eight cabins, the equipment and services, the location and the house rules, and moves the visitor to direct contact (WhatsApp first, then email and Instagram) to check availability and rates. Success: visitors contact the complex directly instead of booking through a third party, already knowing which cabin category fits their group.

## Positioning

A small complex of eight cabins with the comfort and service of a top-tier hotel and the feeling of being at home. Every unit has a private terrace with panoramic views of the lake (Dique La Angostura) and the mountains, its own grill and its own garage. Each cabin is named after a flower of the Argentine north and Latin America. Guest ratings are among the highest in the valley (see Evidence).

## Product Principles

1. Direct contact is the goal: every section leads naturally to WhatsApp, with the visitor's language and intent pre-filled.
2. The view and the terrace are the product; show them before listing amenities.
3. Help the visitor pick the right cabin for their group size quickly.
4. Real ratings over adjectives: proof comes from guests, not from claims.
5. Clear, honest practical information (rules, check-in, pets, children) so nothing surprises a guest on arrival.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Bilingual ES/EN with correct `lang` attributes. Mobile-first: most visitors arrive on phones from Instagram or Maps. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
